Why Become a Member?

Your membership helps fund spring flower shows, community outreach, and iris education. Members receive exclusive access to sales, workshops, and our newsletter.

  • Free admission to meetings and shows
  • Member-only access to iris sales
  • Monthly newsletter and event updates
  • Support for iris culture and scholarship
